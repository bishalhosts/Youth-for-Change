Drug Awareness

What is addiction?

A health condition where stopping substance use feels very hard despite harm. It is treatable.

Common forms of misuse

Tobacco, alcohol, cannabis, misused medicines and inhalants.

Health risks

Substances can affect the brain, heart and mood, and may worsen anxiety or depression.

Impact on education and family

Missed school, falling grades and strained family trust are common risks.

Peer pressure

It is okay to say “no”. Real friends respect your choices.

Warning signs

Sudden mood changes, withdrawal, falling grades or loss of interest. These have many causes, so respond with care, not blame.

How to prevent it

Build healthy routines, stay active, talk openly and ask for help early.

How parents and teachers can help

Listen without judgment, stay involved and connect young people with professional support.

Report a Concern

Share a concern about substance misuse through a secure external Google Form. Avoid naming individuals unless truly necessary.

Never investigate or confront anyone yourself. Reports are reviewed by authorized, responsible people and referred appropriately.

This is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, call Nepal Police at 100.

Reports are never shown publicly. We cannot promise absolute anonymity, so share only what is needed.

About Us

Youth for Change is a community initiative focused on raising awareness, preventing drug addiction, and supporting the youth of our community. Together, we can build a healthier, stronger and drug-free future.

Mission

Connect young people with information, support and positive opportunities.

Vision

A community where young people are healthy, safe and hopeful.

Objectives

Raise awareness, make confidential support easier to reach, and run positive activities.

Community involvement

Parents, teachers, schools and young people are all welcome to take part.

This is an independent community initiative. It does not claim official registration or partnership with any organization.
